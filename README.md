### CHEM-000447: Chloride-versus-sulfate pathway flux in cinnabar blackening
- **Subfield:** Round 6
- **Open:** Measure contributions of chloride-assisted and sulfate-mediated pathways to HgS conversion into metallic mercury under museum illumination. Close the mercury balance across pigment, binder, secondary salts, and vapor.
- **Status:** Metallic mercury was directly demonstrated in photoaged cinnabar tempera in 2021, including a chloride-free pathway. Relative pathway fluxes under ordinary display conditions remain a proposed narrower target.
- **Type:** experimental
- **Why:** Identifies which environmental intervention slows a particular cinnabar paint.
- **Key works:** K. Elert, M. Pérez Mendoza, and C. Cardell (2021), “Direct evidence for metallic mercury causing photo-induced darkening of red cinnabar tempera paints,” [DOI: 10.1038/s42004-021-00610-2](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42004-021-00610-2).
- **Related:** Environmental science—reactive-mercury source attribution; inverse problems—parallel reaction rates.
